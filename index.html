THE RENDERER

Light, not a filter.

WGSL runs through vGPU directly on WebGPU. Signed-distance geometry is lit with GGX microfacet shading, inverse-square sources, visibility rays, and single-scattering volume integration.

These are real-time approximations. Geometry and its looping deformation are authored, not a physics simulation. There is no path tracing, indirect bounce lighting, or simulated combustion.

Colors are authored and saved exclusively in OKLCH. They are gamut-mapped into linear radiance for lighting, then display-encoded once. PNG output is sRGB.

A seed, recipe, shader revision, output resolution, quality, and exact time reproduce a study on the same renderer. Different GPUs can differ by small floating-point and antialiasing amounts.

MIT ports: Ashima simplex noise and Hugh Kennedy's ordered dithering. Attribution and source hashes are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
